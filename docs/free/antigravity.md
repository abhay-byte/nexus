## What Free Actually Gets You

- Access to Gemini 3.1 Pro AND Claude Opus in one IDE (separate quotas)
- 5 parallel AI agents running simultaneously
- Agent Skills for custom workflows
- Built-in browser for web testing
- Completely free — no credit card required

## What Free Does NOT Include

- Sufficient quota for heavy daily use (92% cut)
- Priority during high demand (Pro lockouts reported 7+ days)
- Transparent credit system
- Reliable Gemini quality (reports of "lobotomized" behavior)

## Warning

Google cut the free tier by 92% since launch. The free tier exists but you will hit rate limits fast.
